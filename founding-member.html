Founding Member Cohort

Year-round coaching for mountain athletes who want clear structure, accountability, and season-to-season progression without starting from zero each year.

$99/month founding rate 3-month initial commitment Limited cohort spots

Interested in joining the cohort? Email joe@nomadicperformance.com.

Who This Is For

  • Mountain athletes juggling multiple sports across seasons
  • Athletes who want more than a static workout PDF
  • People who want direct support and accountability
  • Athletes who want injury-risk reduction while improving performance

What Is Included

  • Initial performance and movement assessment
  • Training and injury history review
  • Personalized training roadmap
  • Individualized strength, mobility, and corrective programming
  • Monthly progress reviews and updates
  • Ongoing messaging support for modifications and questions
  • Quarterly reassessments

What It Is Not

  • Not a generic one-size-fits-all plan
  • Not medical care in place of in-person evaluation when required
  • Not a "set it and forget it" program without follow-up
  • Not just preseason prep with no in-season support

How the First 3 Months Work

  1. Month 1: Assessment, history review, baseline plan setup.
  2. Month 2: Progression, workload tuning, recovery and technique emphasis.
  3. Month 3: Reassessment, adjustment, and long-term direction planning.

After the initial 3 months, you can continue month-to-month at the same founding rate while your membership remains active.
