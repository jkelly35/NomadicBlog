Hey, I'm Jamie!

Full-time traveller, part-time writer, perpetual coffee drinker.

I quit my desk job in 2019 with a one-way ticket and a carry-on bag, determined to see as much of the world as possible before settling down. What started as a three-month sabbatical turned into a lifestyle I never want to give up.

On this blog I share honest travel guides, budget breakdowns, solo travel safety tips, and the stories that don't make it onto Instagram – the missed buses, wrong turns, and unexpected friendships that make travel so worthwhile.

When I'm not writing, you'll probably find me learning a few words of the local language, hunting for the best street food market, or staring at a map wondering where to go next.
